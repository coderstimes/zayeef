# Ahmed Zayeef

Ahmed Zayeef is a PhD candidate in Information and Media in the School of Journalism at Michigan State University. He studies journalism as a negotiated practice, examining how journalists manage the conflicts, uncertainties, and ambiguities that emerge when professional ideals encounter institutional, political, and community pressures. His research lies at the intersection of journalism studies, media sociology, political communication, and media ethics.
